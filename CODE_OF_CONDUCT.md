# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Participation must remain free
from harassment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual and romantic identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and requests to stop an interaction.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.
- Give credit and disclose meaningful conflicts of interest.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
doxxing, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, and retaliation against someone who
reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/thomhurst/Kevlar/security/advisories/new). Include links
or screenshots when available. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is harmful. Enforcement decisions consider impact, context, recurrence,
and willingness to repair harm. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents Kevlar elsewhere.
Questions about the policy may be raised privately with the maintainers.
